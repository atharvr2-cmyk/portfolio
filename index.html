About me

Engineering flight through design, analysis, and hands-on work.

I’m an Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign with interests in aerodynamics, autonomous flight, structures, and the tools that connect an engineering concept to a physical system.

My work spans parametric CAD, mechanical assemblies, UAV development, composite structures, and Python-based engineering analysis. I founded UAV@Illinois and contribute to hands-on aerospace work through the Illinois Space Society and other student engineering teams.

I’m also developing AeroDesignX, an aircraft-design platform combining mission evaluation, configuration optimization, automated CAD generation, and aerodynamic analysis.

Constructed and assembled a detailed representation of the McLaren MP4/4 using Siemens NX. The project required individual component modeling, surface-led geometry, feature organization, mirrored construction, visual detailing, and integration of the completed components into a full vehicle assembly.

Selected work · 02

Quadcopter Assembly

A multicomponent UAV assembly developed in SolidWorks to practice detailed part modeling, mechanical packaging, fastener placement, and complete assembly integration.

Platform
SolidWorks
Project type
CAD assembly
Focus
Mechanical integration
Status
Complete

Modeled nearly all individual components and constructed the complete quadcopter assembly using supplied design references. The assembly includes a removable structural enclosure, four truss-style arms, motor and propeller hardware, internal component packaging, landing supports, side panels, and detailed fastener placement. Exploded configurations were created to document component relationships and assembly order.

Contribution: modeled nearly all components and completed the full assembly. A small number of components and the original project references were supplied.
